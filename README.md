### How do I choose between free and paid podcast hosting?

Free hosting works if you're testing podcasting or producing occasional episodes, but paid plans unlock unlimited storage, better analytics, and monetization tools worth the investment once you're committed. Most creators should start free, then upgrade when storage limits or feature restrictions become frustrating.

### What analytics actually matter for podcasters?

Focus on total downloads, geographic location of listeners, and which episodes retain attention best. Device and app data helps optimize audio quality, while listener drop-off points show where content loses people. Ignore vanity metrics and concentrate on trends over time rather than individual episode spikes.

### Can I switch podcast hosts without losing subscribers?

Yes, through RSS feed forwarding where your old host redirects listeners to the new one automatically. Most platforms offer import tools that migrate episode data, show notes, and artwork in one process. The transition typically takes two weeks for all directories to update their references.
